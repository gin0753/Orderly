[Per-file review](c4-privacy-review.json) classifies all 1,060 original manifest records and the seven Phase 1 planning documents. Only approved public originals enter the explicit path list. All 309 previous candidate files were reassessed; non-image candidates were rescanned with UTF-8/BOM/UTF-16LE decoding. Findings contain rule names and paths, never values.

One text file triggered the additional signature/local-path scan. Their values are not reproduced; originals remain restricted or manual review. Raw scans, structured payloads, logs and traces are preserved externally rather than published wholesale. No trace frames, cookie/session payloads or OAuth data are selected for Git. 25 selected screenshots have bounded visual/metadata review; no visible contact-bearing screenshots enter this proposal. No OCR or exhaustive opaque trace certification is claimed.

Unresolved owner decisions: synthetic contact fixture consent and realistic phone ownership; local user-path publication in the original execution report; wider sharing of unreviewed trace frames, older identity/credential-like logs and production diagnostics; wider archive access, backup/restore and long-term retention policy. The approved private local bundle excludes other local users through protected Windows ACLs; ACLs are not encryption or a tested backup. These do not authorize publishing unresolved evidence. The pre-existing example credential finding is outside C4 and remains for separate owner review.

Approved means eligible for this proposed inventory, not permission to commit or publish. Archive access must remain restricted even for approved image copies bundled with unresolved material.
